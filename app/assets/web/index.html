How to use

Saving: every change (names, races, marks, lines, gun time, corrections) saves the moment you make it. The line under the title says when it last saved, and warns you if the dock couldn't be reached. It keeps trying until it gets through.

Load a session from the dock, or drop GPX/CSV files anywhere on the map.

Names: puck numbers never change (P3 is always P3, always the same colour). Tap a name to say who had that puck today. It's saved for this session only.

Races: Auto split finds the breaks between races, or add them by hand with start and end times. Everything follows the race you pick.

Wind: type the direction it blows from, or press Estimate. You then get VMG, and tacks, gybes and mark roundings told apart.

Tacks & gybes: tap it under a boat, filter, sort Worst first, tap one to jump the replay there. Loss is metres of VMG lost until the boat's back to 95% of its speed before.

Course marks: Add mark, tap the map where it was laid, drag to adjust. Tap a mark to rename it or set the side: red = leave to port, green = leave to starboard. Marks are per race; new races copy the last race's marks.

Start and finish lines: Add start line, tap the committee boat end, then the pin end. Drag either end to adjust. A line can be start, finish or both. Put in the gun time (or use the estimate) to get seconds late, distance back at the gun and OCS. The finish gives order and elapsed time. Tap a boat in the results to watch its start.

Colours: "By tack" colours tracks red on port tack, green on starboard. In the list, →Stbd / →Port is the tack a boat ended up on.

Wrong call? Use the drop-down on any tack, gybe or mark to change it, or pick "Not one". Your corrections are saved with the session.

Phone tracks: on the dock, "Phone upload" shows QR codes: one joins the WiFi, one opens the upload page. On the big screen the upload code sits in the corner. New tracks appear by themselves.

Projector: B for big screen, F for full screen, Loop to keep replaying. Space plays and pauses.

Align starts lines everyone up from when they first go over 1.5 kn, to race a mate from a different day or yourself from last week.
