How do I get a GPX file?
  • Open GPX Tracker (iPhone) or GPS Logger (Android): stop recording, then Share or Export as GPX and save it to Files.
  • Strava: open the activity on the Strava website, then the three dots, then Export GPX.
  • Garmin: in Garmin Connect on the web, open the activity, then the cog, then Export to GPX.
  • Most sailing apps have an Export or Share option. Pick GPX.
